feat(org-classroom): 新增 OrgClassroom 组织工位平面。一人一张单人桌，同一行的桌子拼成长条，按部门分区；座位是 Blender 预渲染的等距精灵图，拆成阴影、颜色组遮罩和白模光照三层，遮罩填主题色、光照用 multiply 叠上，所以人跟 `--color-foreground`、桌面跟 `--color-surface`、围巾跟分区 `accent`，深色模式不用重新出图；图片以 WebP data URI 打进源码，零运行时依赖、不需要 WebGL。点击按像素判断：点到桌椅人身上选中座位，点到透明处落到分区地板并聚焦分区，前后重叠的座位不会互相抢点击。座位是真按钮，Tab 按排列顺序走，聚焦和选中都有描边光晕，Enter 打开人员卡；人员卡里改状态，关闭按钮或 Esc 关闭并把焦点还给座位。排布按组件自身宽度切换，场景缩小后人名气泡收成状态圆点。`Zone.facing` 设定每个分区的人面朝平面图哪边，`view` / `onViewChange` 加指南针旁的两颗按钮旋转观察视角，`northDeg` 让指南针指向真北；指南针画成地面上的圆的投影，和地板同一个透视。默认状态文案锁定为「在岗 / 忙碌 / 会议中 / 休假 / 面试中」。这一版没有拖拽换座、平移缩放、KPI 栏和面试间。

feat(org-classroom): adds OrgClassroom, an organization seat floor. One person per single desk, desks in a row join into a long table, and seats are grouped by department zone. Seats are Blender pre-rendered isometric sprites split into a shadow, color-group masks, and a white shading pass; masks are filled with theme colors and the shading is multiplied on top, so the person follows `--color-foreground`, the desk top follows `--color-surface`, the scarf follows the zone `accent`, and dark mode needs no new images. The images ship as WebP data URIs in source: no runtime dependency and no WebGL. Clicks are tested per pixel: the desk, chair, or person selects the seat, and a transparent spot falls through to the zone floor and focuses the zone, so overlapping neighbors never steal a click. Seats are real buttons; Tab follows row and column order, focus and selection draw an outline glow, and Enter opens the person card. Status changes from the person card; its close button or Escape closes it and returns focus to the seat. The layout follows the component's own width, and name bubbles collapse into status dots when the scene is scaled down. `Zone.facing` sets which side of the floor plan each zone faces, `view` / `onViewChange` plus two buttons next to the compass rotate the viewing angle, and `northDeg` points the compass at true north; the compass is drawn as a circle on the floor, in the same perspective as the floor. Default status labels stay the locked Chinese copy. This version has no drag-to-reseat, pan/zoom, KPI bar, or interview room.
