新增 NovelReader 与 NovelEditor。阅读进度只记章节和字符偏移；翻页（滚动 / 覆盖 / 仿真）、字号、行距、栏宽和纸色收在同一套底部设置里，菜单默认不占位。章首放上一章、章末放下一章，都在正文流里；第一章和最后一章不放用不到的那一枚。不靠整页常驻顶底栏。

<!-- changelog-en:start -->
Adds NovelReader and NovelEditor. Reading progress stores only the chapter and a character offset. Page turns (scroll, cover, or simulation), type size, line height, column width, and paper color live in one bottom settings sheet, and the menu does not take space by default. The previous chapter sits at the start of the chapter and the next chapter at the end, both in the text flow; the first and last chapters omit the control that would do nothing. It does not rely on top and bottom bars that stay on the page.
<!-- changelog-en:end -->
